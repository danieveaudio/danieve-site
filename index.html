Sometimes one session isn't the thing. Sometimes you need someone to actually sit inside the problem with you, figure out what's really going on, and build the structure around it. That's Deep Dive.

Phase 1: Discovery ()

I research your space before we ever meet. Then we have one or two real conversations (90 minutes each) where we do the diagnostic work. What are you actually offering? Who is it for? What's getting in the way? If you're fuzzy on direction, we do that work first. (No shame. Most people are.)

You walk away with clarity on your core offer, a diagnosis of what's actually blocking progress (often not what you thought), and a 90-day plan built around your real life.

Phase 2: The Build (from )

This is where we build the actual thing. Roadmap, templates, outreach scripts, pricing structures, launch plans. Done with you or done for you, depending on what fits.

Phase 2 gets scoped after Phase 1, because I won't quote what I don't know yet. You pay for exactly what you need. Nothing you don't.

Real examples: one client walked out with a trade program and outreach scripts for interior designers, a collection launch checklist, and a loyalty loop. Another got a concierge gifting starter kit, a pricing framework, and a warm outreach approach for her first five clients. The stuff that actually moves the needle, built for your specific situation.
